Privacy Policy

FDC Instagram Poster · Last updated: 23 October 2026

1. Who we are

This policy covers the "FDC Instagram Poster" app (the "App"), operated by FDC Cosmetics & Whitening, part of Family Dental Centre, 404 Kingstanding Road, Birmingham, B44 8LD, United Kingdom ("we", "us").

Contact: appointments@family-dental-centre.co.uk or 0121 382 2880.

2. What the App does

The App is a private, internal tool. Once a week it creates a marketing post (an AI-generated illustrative image and a caption) and publishes it to our own Instagram business account, @fdc.dental, using the Instagram API with Instagram Login. The App is used only by our practice team. It is not offered to patients, followers or the public, and it has no public sign-up.

3. Information the App handles

The App only handles information about our own Instagram account and our own marketing content:

  • Instagram account access token for @fdc.dental, which lets the App publish on our behalf.
  • Basic account details of our own account (such as the username and account ID), used to confirm the App is connected to the correct account.
  • Post content we create: the generated image, caption, hashtags and alt text.
  • Approver email address: the work email address of the one team member who approves posts, used only to send them the weekly draft for approval.

The App does not collect, read or store personal information about our patients, followers, visitors or any other Instagram users. It does not access followers' profiles, comments, direct messages or insights. It does not use patient images, records or any health information.

4. How we use information

We use the information above only to generate and publish our own marketing posts on Instagram and to keep the connection to our account working (including refreshing the access token before it expires). We do not use it for advertising profiles, automated decision-making or any other purpose, and we do not sell it.

6. Storage and retention

  • The access token is stored as an encrypted secret and is kept until we revoke it or remove the App.
  • Draft posts are kept as workflow records on GitHub for 7 days and then deleted. Approval links expire after 5 days, and the Cloudflare service keeps only a short record that a link has been used (no post content), for up to 14 days.
  • Published posts remain on our Instagram account until we delete them.

7. Security

Access to the App and its credentials is restricted to authorised members of our team. Credentials are never written into the source code, and nothing is posted until a member of staff approves a specific caption from the weekly approval email.

8. Your rights

Because the App does not collect personal information about patients, followers or other individuals, there is generally no such data for us to access, correct or delete. If you believe we hold personal information about you in connection with the App, you have rights under the UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, including access, correction and erasure. Contact us using the details above and we will respond within one month. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

9. Data deletion instructions

The App does not store personal data about Instagram users other than the account owner. To remove the App's access to the @fdc.dental account, or to request deletion of any data connected to it:

  • In the Instagram app, go to Settings > Website permissions > Apps and websites, find "FDC Instagram Poster" and choose Remove. This revokes the App's access token.
  • Or email appointments@family-dental-centre.co.uk with the subject "Data deletion request". We will delete any stored data connected to your request and confirm by email within 30 days.
